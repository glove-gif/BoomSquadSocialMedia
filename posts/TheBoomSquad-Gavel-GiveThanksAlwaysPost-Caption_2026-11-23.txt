Thankful on the good days AND the hard days! 🙌

"give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you." 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (ESV)

Gavel knows a secret: thankful hearts are strong hearts. Even when things go wrong, God is still good and still with us.

Parents: tonight, ask your kids for one "hard thing" and one "good thing" from today, then thank God for both together.

Share this with a family getting ready for Thanksgiving!

What's one thing you can thank God for even on a hard day? Tell us below!
