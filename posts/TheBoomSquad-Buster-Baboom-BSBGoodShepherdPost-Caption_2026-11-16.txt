Who takes care of you? 🐑

"The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." Psalm 23:1 (ESV)

Buster and Baboom are pointing to the One who watches over us every day. Just like a good shepherd cares for his sheep, Jesus knows your name and takes care of you!

Parents: say this verse together at breakfast and ask, "How did God take care of us this week?"
