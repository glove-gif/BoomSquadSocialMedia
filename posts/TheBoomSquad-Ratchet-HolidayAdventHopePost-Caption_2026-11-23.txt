Advent starts today! 🕯️

"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope." Romans 15:13 (ESV)

Today we light the first Advent candle: the candle of hope! Ratchet is cheering, because Jesus came to bring hope to the whole world. Let's get our hearts ready for Christmas!

Parents: light a candle at dinner tonight, read this verse together and ask, "What are we hoping God will do this Christmas?"
