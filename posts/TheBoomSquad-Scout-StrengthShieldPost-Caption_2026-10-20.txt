Shield up, heroes! 🛡️

"The LORD is my strength and my shield; in him my heart trusts, and I am helped..." Psalm 28:7 (ESV)

Scout knows her shield is strong, but she knows Someone even stronger. When your kids feel small, scared, or worn out, remind them that God Himself is their strength and their shield. They can trust Him, and He helps!

Share this with a family who needs a little courage today, or tag a young hero you love.
