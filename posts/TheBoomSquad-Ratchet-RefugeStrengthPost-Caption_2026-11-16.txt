Need a safe place? God is right there! 🛡️

"God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble." Psalm 46:1 (ESV)

Ratchet knows that real strength doesn't come from a hologram shield. It comes from God! When something feels scary or hard, kids can run to Him in prayer anytime, anywhere.

Parents: read this verse at bedtime and ask, "What's one thing you want to give to God tonight?"
